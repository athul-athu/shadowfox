Our Story

About Tanjore Degree Coffee

Welcome to Tanjore

In January 22, 2013, Shri C. Saminathan, with over 35 years of experience in the FMCG industry, laid the foundation for his coffee decoction venture under the brand name 'TANJORE Degree Coffee'.

For generations, Tanjore Degree Coffee has been synonymous with authentic, traditional coffee prepared with love and care. Our journey began with a simple mission: to bring the rich, aromatic experience of true South Indian coffee to coffee lovers everywhere.

Authenticity

Our commitment to preserving the authentic taste and aroma of South Indian filter coffee sets us apart from the rest.

Quality

From carefully selected beans to meticulous brewing techniques, we ensure superior coffee experience.
